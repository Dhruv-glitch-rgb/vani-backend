1

Data Sovereignty & Local Storage (E:\BoVxAi DB)

V.A.N.I-xAI is engineered with indigenous data sovereignty at its foundational core. While lightweight metadata, text chat pairing, and WebRTC handshakes are coordinated securely via Firestore, all heavy media assets (including user voice recordings, captured photos, camera snapshots, and Quantum Beam peer-to-peer file drops) are stored locally in the sovereign partition E:\BoVxAi DB partitioned strictly by your mathematical V.A.N.I-xAI User ID.

High-bandwidth media is never uploaded to third-party public cloud storage or monetized for advertising purposes.

2

Local Terminal Logs, Voice Audio & Screenshots

Logs printed to the terminal console are stored in volatile local buffers for live rendering in the UI. Taken screenshots and recorded voice notes are saved directly into your isolated user folder on E:\BoVxAi DB\<V.A.N.I-xAI-USER-ID>\. They remain under your sovereign control and can be viewed, exported, or permanently erased at any time.

3

Third-Party AI APIs (Google Gemini & LLMs)

When you enter a custom Google Gemini API Key, queries that you type or speak are transmitted directly from your client environment to Google's Generative AI endpoint to parse intent parameters. The storage, security, and usage of that query text are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. V.A.N.I-xAI does not intercept or warehouse your private API secret keys.

4

ADB & Android Debug Hardware Security

Android mobile integration operates entirely over a direct USB cable link using local ADB commands. You maintain explicit cryptographic control over which physical devices are authorized via RSA prompt on your smartphone. No remote telemetry server or third-party connections are initiated during ADB shell operations.

5

Sandboxed Browser Storage (localStorage)

The web console preserves your runtime interface preferences (such as theme preferences, optional API key values, and local sound effects toggles) inside your browser's sandboxed localStorage. This data never leaves your client environment and is automatically purged upon browser cache clearing.

6

Quantum Beam & Peer-to-Peer Privacy

Peer-to-peer data transfers initiated through Quantum Beam utilize direct WebRTC data channels with DTLS/SRTP hardware-level encryption. The signaling server facilitates the initial connection handshake only; file contents stream peer-to-peer and are saved directly into your sovereign E:\BoVxAi DB directory.

7

Your Sovereign Rights & Data Deletion

You retain the fundamental right to:

  • Audit all stored media directly by browsing your local directory E:\BoVxAi DB\<YOUR-USER-ID>\.
  • Purge all locally cached data at any time with zero remote friction.
  • Opt out of cloud synchronization features and run completely in offline or local-model LLM modes.
