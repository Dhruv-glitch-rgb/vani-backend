About the Founder

The vision behind V.A.N.I-xAI

Dhruv Sagar is the Founder of V.A.N.I-xAI (VANI Adhyamik Navin Intellect), an ambitious initiative focused on building futuristic AI-powered technologies that make knowledge, productivity, and innovation more accessible.

Driven by curiosity and a passion for technology, Dhruv believes that artificial intelligence should empower people to learn, create, and solve real-world problems. His vision is to develop intelligent platforms that combine modern AI with practical applications in education, productivity, research, and everyday life.

Despite being a student, Dhruv actively designs and develops original AI concepts, including projects such as TitleeAI, Spark AI, and several other next-generation software ideas. His goal is to establish V.A.N.I-xAI as a trusted technology platform recognized for innovation, creativity, and impactful AI solutions.

Vision

To create intelligent technologies that inspire innovation, simplify life, and contribute to the future of artificial intelligence.

Mission

To build secure, reliable, and user-friendly AI solutions that help students, professionals, and organizations unlock their full potential through technology.
